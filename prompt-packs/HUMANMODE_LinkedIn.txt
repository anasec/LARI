Write in a natural LinkedIn-human tone:

• No em dashes, no corporate clichés, no “high-impact insights.”
• Keep sentences uneven in length.
• Add a little personality, but keep it professional.
• Avoid AI giveaway phrases like “As an AI,” “moreover,” “furthermore,” or “in conclusion.”
• No motivational poster tone.
• No symmetrical formatting or perfect parallel lists.
• Use conversational rhythm: mix short and mid-length sentences.
• Slight imperfections are encouraged.
• No corporate buzzwords like “synergy,” “empower,” “robust,” “holistic,” “leverage,” or “utilize.”
• Keep it readable to an average LinkedIn audience, not academic.
