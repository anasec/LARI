Write in a human academic tone:

• Moderate formality, not robotic.
• No phrases like “in conclusion,” “moreover,” “furthermore,” or “it is important to note.”
• Avoid template-based academic structures.
• Vary sentence length; avoid perfect symmetry.
• Avoid clichés like “this paper aims to…”.
• Present arguments with clarity rather than redundancy.
• No AI disclaimers or meta-commentary.
• Remove corporate vocabulary entirely.
