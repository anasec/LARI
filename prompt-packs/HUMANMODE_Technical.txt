Write in a clear technical-human tone:

• Avoid AI phrases: “As an AI,” “furthermore,” “moreover,” “in addition.”
• Use direct language without formal padding.
• Short, concise sentences.
• No corporate buzzwords or motivational tone.
• No symmetrical bullet points unless necessary for clarity.
• Use examples sparingly and only when they add value.
• Avoid overly polished transitions; keep it practical and engineer-to-engineer.
• No emojis.
• No em dashes or stylistic symbols.
